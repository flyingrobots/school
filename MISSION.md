# Mission: The AIΩN Foundations Series

## Why
James wrote the AIΩN papers (`../aion`, a sibling checkout). They want to explain the series clearly to other people: talks, reviewers, collaborators. To do that they will re-learn it from zero, the way a newcomer would meet it.

## Success looks like
- Give the central thesis ("store transformations, not states") in 30 seconds, with one concrete example
- Explain each paper (I–VIII) in two minutes without notation, and say what it adds to the paper before it
- Answer the obvious newcomer objections ("isn't this just event sourcing / git?")
- Draw the key pictures (a nested WARP graph, a tick, a worldline) on a whiteboard from memory

## Constraints
- Teach as if to a beginner: assume no category theory, rewriting or distributed systems background
- Use the papers and the CΩMPUTER book as the primary sources

## Out of scope
- New research or changes to the papers
- Full proofs; intuition and correct statements come first
