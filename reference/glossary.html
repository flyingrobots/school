AIΩN Glossary

Terms are added only when a lesson teaches them. Use these words exactly.

TermPlain meaningSource
StateWhat the system looks like at one moment: a snapshot.README
TransformationA recorded change that turns one state into the next.README
The inversionThe series' central thesis: make the transformation, not the state, the thing you store. You derive state by replaying the history.README, "Central Thesis"
Witnessed causal historyThe record of transformations and what caused each one. AIΩN treats this record as the primary thing; graphs, runtimes and systems are readings of it.README, Paper VIII
Deterministic replayRunning the same history again always gives the same state.Paper II
WARP graph (WARP)Worldline Algebra for Recursive Provenance. Either an atom, or a skeleton graph whose every vertex and edge carries another WARP.Paper I, Def. 1
AtomRaw data a WARP doesn't look inside (number, string, ID). Depth 0.Paper I, §2
SkeletonThe ordinary directed graph (dots and arrows) at one level of a WARP.Paper I, Def. 1
AttachmentThe WARP carried by a vertex or an edge of a skeleton.Paper I, Def. 1
DepthHow many levels of nesting a WARP has; atoms are 0, a skeleton is 1 + its deepest attachment.Paper I, “Depth”
Well-foundedNesting always ends in atoms after finitely many levels.Paper I, §2
